By Joseph Samara, founder of NuFlow, an AI automation and marketing agency in San Pedro Sula, Honduras. November 2026. Read the full report in Spanish.

AI adoption among small and medium businesses in Honduras is just starting. According to ECLAC (CEPAL), only 5.4% of Honduran companies actively use artificial intelligence, compared with 9.1% across Latin America and 20.2% in OECD countries. Meanwhile, 8 million Hondurans are online. The gap is not access. It is use.

Three findings from the field

1. Honduras buys through WhatsApp. Customers ask for prices, availability and orders by message, even when a business sends them a booking link. The practical question for an SMB is how to answer on WhatsApp in seconds, at any hour, without hiring three people. A WhatsApp AI agent handles the repetitive questions and hands the rest to a person.

2. Businesses post a lot and measure little. Most SMBs publish the same content on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, although each network has a different audience and a different winning format. A monthly social media analytics review answers three questions: which post started the most conversations, which format reached the most people on each network, and what to repeat next month.

3. The owner is the bottleneck. In a Honduran SMB the owner is often the salesperson, community manager and customer service desk at once. Automation does not replace people. It gives the owner hours back.

A 90-day roadmap

Days 1 to 30, measure: a digital presence analysis of which network brings customers, which content works and how long it takes to reply. Days 31 to 60, automate the repetitive: an agent for frequent WhatsApp questions and a CRM that captures every conversation. Days 61 to 90, grow: a content calendar based on what worked, campaigns to past customers and a monthly results report.
